## 2. Page Architecture

### TOP HALF — Landing Page

**Hero**
- Headline: `The view you were promised.`
- Sub-line: `RailNest is the only scenic heritage rail operator that shows you exactly which car, which window, and which direction you'll face — before you book.`
- Primary CTA: `Explore Routes & Seats →`
- Visual: Interactive cross-section of train car types — each labelled, hoverable, expandable

**Three Reasons This Is For You**
1. `No seat surprises.` Every car on every route has a plain-English description, a window diagram, and a direction-of-travel indicator. What you see is what you get.
2. `The middle track.` More curated than Amtrak. More accessible than Rocky Mountaineer. Real scenic rail, real-world pricing — $175 to $900 per person.
3. `Southwest at human speed.` Four routes through the American Southwest and Pacific Coast. No airports. No layovers. Just landscape, moving.

## 3. Voice and Tone

**Three adjectives:** Transparent · Unhurried · Assured

**Use:** "The view you were promised" / "Know before you board" / "Your car. Your window. Your journey."
**Never use:** "World-class" / "Luxury at its finest" / "Don't miss out"

---

## 4. Visual Direction

**Colour mood:** Midnight rail blue → warm amber → canyon terracotta. Dark-first.

**Typography:**
- Display: Playfair Display (editorial, heritage)
- Body: Inter (clean, operationally clear)

**Signature element:** Interactive train car cross-section diagram in Hero — 5 car types, each clickable to expand description, window type, and price tier. Directly addresses the seat-confusion pain point at the moment of landing.
