The fleet

Know your vantage point before you board.

Heritage cars are not interchangeable. Seat orientation, window dimensions, and cabin type vary dramatically car-to-car. We map every detail so you choose the seat, not just the train.

01

Vista Dome

360° panoramic glass ceilings — best for the Sierra climb and canyon rim approach.

02

Sunset Parlor

Swivel armchairs oriented west for Pacific sunsets. Private reservation only.

03

Pullman Sleeper

Restored 1948 berths with full-height bay windows. Overnight routes only.

04

Open Observation

Brass-railed rear deck — open to weather, open to view. Seasonal availability.

Our philosophy

"The rhythm of the rails is the last refuge of the contemplative traveller."

Every reservation supports the operators preserving these routes — their restoration shops, their archives, and the slow, deliberate craft of keeping a 1940s observation car on the rails for another century.

Before you board

Questions we know you'll ask.

Your car type, position, direction of travel, and window size are confirmed in your booking receipt and on your ticket. If anything changes before departure, we notify you within 24 hours and offer a free seat swap or a full refund — your choice.
The Vista Dome is a shared open car with 360° panoramic glass — social, day-use, best for mountain and canyon passes. The Pullman Sleeper is a private enclosed berth with full-height bay windows, converting from seating to a full bed for overnight routes. Different products at different prices, clearly labelled before you book.
Yes — free if requested 48 or more hours before departure. A $25 admin fee applies between 24–48 hours. No changes inside 24 hours, as cars are sealed and manifests locked for operations.
Every ticket listing specifies seat type, whether meal service is included, shared vs. private car, direction of travel, and available add-ons. Wine, spirits, and dining upgrades are itemised separately. Nothing appears as a surprise line item at checkout.
Full refund if cancelled 7 or more days before departure. 50% refund for cancellations 3–7 days before. No refund inside 3 days, though you may transfer your booking to another traveller at no charge.
